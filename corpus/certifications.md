Ben holds the AWS Certified AI Practitioner certification, AIF-C01, issued by Amazon Web Services
in 2025.

It is the foundational-tier AI and machine learning certification in the AWS track, covering
generative AI concepts, foundation models, prompt engineering, responsible AI practice, and the
AWS services that sit around them such as Bedrock and SageMaker.

He is straightforward about what a foundational certification does and does not evidence. It
demonstrates a working vocabulary and a structured grounding in the concepts, which is genuinely
useful for talking to stakeholders about what a model can and cannot be trusted to do. It is not
a substitute for having built something, which is the gap that projects like this question
answering service are meant to close. The certification came first and the applied work followed,
in that order, and he would rather be asked about the second.
