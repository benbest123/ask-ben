On AWS, Ben has worked with Glue, CDK, S3, CloudFormation, IAM, CodePipeline, Athena and KMS,
mostly in combination rather than in isolation, building the partner billing pipeline at Visa
where the ETL ran on Glue and the whole per-partner infrastructure was defined in CDK.

His DevOps tooling is Git, GitHub Actions, CI/CD and infrastructure as code. Every one of his own
projects runs lint, type checks and tests on each pull request, a habit carried over from the
Visa work rather than something done for display.

On the BI side he has used Power BI, Qlik and SSRS in production. Power BI most deeply, from
building self-service dashboards for around three hundred lawyers at Russell McVeagh; Qlik from
the embedding and infrastructure work at Visa; SSRS mostly as the legacy system he was replacing.

Infrastructure as code is the piece he would defend hardest of these. The per-partner isolation
on the billing pipeline was only reviewable because it was written as code.
