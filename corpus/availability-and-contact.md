Ben is available for work and is based in London, with full right to work in the UK.

The best routes to him are his LinkedIn profile at linkedin.com/in/benjamin-s-best and his GitHub
at github.com/benbest123. His email address is on the Contact window of his site at benbest.uk.

That last detail is deliberate rather than evasive, and the reasoning is the kind of thing this
widget exists to demonstrate. This is a public endpoint that answers questions on request, which
makes it a convenient thing to scrape. Contact details that a bot will emit to anyone who asks
are contact details that end up in bulk mailing lists, so the corpus behind this widget contains
no email address and no number, and a test in the repository fails the build if one is ever added.
A human visitor is one click away from the same information.

Anything about notice periods, availability dates or compensation is better discussed directly
than answered by a language model working from notes.
