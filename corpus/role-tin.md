Ben was a Research Analyst at Technology Investment Network in New Zealand from November 2021 to
June 2022, his first role after graduating.

He automated the data-collection process in Python, replacing legacy Excel queries and manual
data entry, and produced the data analysis and written content that fed the published reports on
the New Zealand technology sector.

It is the smallest role on his CV and the one that set the pattern for the rest. The work he was
hired to do was largely manual assembly, and the useful contribution turned out to be removing
most of it rather than doing it faster. That is also where the writing half of the job came in.
The reports were published, so the analysis had to be defensible to readers who had never seen
the underlying data, which is close to the habit of documenting decisions and their rejected
alternatives that shows up throughout his later projects.
