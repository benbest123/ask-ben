Benjamin Best is a data engineer based in London, with full right to work in the UK. He has four
years of commercial experience across three sectors: financial services at Visa, law at Russell
McVeagh, and technology research at Technology Investment Network.

His range is deliberately end to end. At Visa he worked on cloud infrastructure and ETL at one
end and on the React interfaces that surfaced the resulting data at the other, which is unusual
for a data engineering title and is the thread running through most of his work: he tends to own
a thing from the storage layer through to the screen a user actually looks at.

He is open to data engineering roles and, after roughly the last year at Visa spent in a more
software-focused position, to software engineering ones as well. He is not looking to specialise
narrowly in one half of that. The work he has enjoyed most has involved holding both ends at
once, and the projects he builds in his own time tend to have the same shape.
