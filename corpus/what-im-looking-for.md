Ben is targeting mid-level data engineering roles and junior software engineering roles, and
anything close to either. The software engineering side follows from roughly the last year at
Visa spent in a more software-focused position.

What he is actually optimising for is the shape of the work rather than the title. The work he
has enjoyed most has been end to end: owning something from the cloud infrastructure and the ETL
through to the interface that puts the result in front of a user. Roles that split those apart,
where the data lands in a warehouse and becomes someone else's problem, are a worse fit for him
than the title alone would suggest.

He is drawn to teams that write things down. The pattern running through his own projects is that
every significant decision has its rejected alternative recorded next to it, and he would rather
join somewhere that treats that as normal than somewhere it has to be argued for.

He is based in London and holds a Youth Mobility Visa valid until June 2029, which gives him the
right to work in the UK without sponsorship for that period. Specifics about availability, notice
and compensation are best discussed directly rather than through this widget.
