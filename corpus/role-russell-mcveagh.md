Ben was a Business Intelligence Analyst at Russell McVeagh, a New Zealand law firm, from June
2022 to August 2023. He owned the firm business-intelligence reporting, delivering revenue,
client-activity and time-allocation reporting to senior stakeholders.

The main piece of work was building self-service Power BI dashboards end to end for the firm
roughly three hundred lawyers, replacing legacy SSRS reports. The measurable outcome was the
elimination of recurring ad-hoc requests that had previously required someone to write a manual
SQL extract each time.

That framing is the thing he took from the role. The reporting backlog was not really a reporting
problem. It was that the people asking the questions had no way to answer them without going
through him. Building a dashboard that answers one question well, and building one that lets a
non-technical user answer the next twenty themselves, are quite different design problems, and
only the second one makes the queue go away.
