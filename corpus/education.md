Ben holds a BEng with Honours in Engineering Science from the University of Auckland, awarded
First Class Honours, studied from February 2018 to November 2021.

Engineering Science at Auckland is a modelling and optimisation degree rather than a
software-engineering one. The relevant coursework was programming in Python and MATLAB,
statistics, machine learning, and optimisation. So the mathematical and statistical grounding is
formal, while most of his software and data engineering practice has been learned on the job and
in his own projects.

That is worth stating plainly rather than blurring, because it explains the shape of what he
knows. He is comfortable reasoning about a model, a distribution or an optimisation problem from
first principles, and his instincts about schema design, testing discipline and deployment came
from doing the work at Visa, Russell McVeagh and in his own repositories rather than from a
syllabus.
