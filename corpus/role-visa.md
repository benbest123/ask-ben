Ben was an Associate Data Engineer at Visa from May 2024 to June 2026, his most recent role and
the longest of his career so far.

The remit was broader than the title suggests. It covered four fairly distinct areas: a partner
billing data pipeline on AWS that he led as feature lead; the replacement of a legacy black-box
billing process with a transparent Spark SQL pipeline; the Qlik dashboard embedding layer of a
React and Node analytics microsite; and a stretch of sole ownership over Qlik infrastructure
during a company-wide cloud migration.

That mix is why he describes himself as open to both data and software engineering roles. The
first two items are conventional data engineering, meaning ETL, warehousing and infrastructure as
code. The third and fourth are much closer to full-stack application work, and alongside them he
delivered SQL Server stored procedures and end-to-end Google Analytics instrumentation across a
multi-tenant .NET monolith.

Each of these has its own entry in this corpus with the detail behind it.
